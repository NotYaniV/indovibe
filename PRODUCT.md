# Product

IndoVibe: modular kitchens, wardrobes, TV units and custom modular furniture, designed and manufactured in Goa, India.

## Platform
web

## Stack
Static HTML/CSS/JS single page in `indovibe-redesign/` (index.html, style.css, script.js). No build step. Replaces the current WordPress (Kitchor theme) one-pager at indovibe.in. Source content scraped to `../indovibe-scrape/pages/home.md`; imagery in `../indovibe-scrape/images/_homepage/`.

## Users
- Goa homeowners building or renovating (villas, apartments, second homes) choosing a kitchen or wardrobe maker.
- Design-conscious buyers comparing modular brands; they want to touch finishes before committing.
- Kanpur (UP) homeowners, via the second experience centre.
- Job seekers: interior designers and project managers in Goa.

## Product Purpose
Get the visitor to book a consultation or walk into the Porvorim Experience Centre. The site must prove taste (design), control (own factory), and care (named people).

## Positioning
"You dream it, we design it." Everything modular, designed around the client's life, built in IndoVibe's own Pilerne factory with European machinery, installed by in-house teams. Contemporary India, not an "Indian theme".

Name "IndoVibe" / "INDOVIBE". Existing logo PNGs in scrape. Keep all current homepage sections (user instruction). Copy tightened to luxury length; every fact kept, nothing invented.

## Product Principles
- The showroom visit is the conversion; every path leads to it or to WhatsApp.
- Craft proof over adjectives: factory, hands, named people.
- Less UI, more atmosphere. Slow, fluid motion.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
